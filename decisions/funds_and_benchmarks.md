# Funds and benchmarks
Funds: Akre (13F CIK 1112520, NAV AKRIX), Jensen (1106129, JENIX) against IVV; Polen (1034524, POLIX) against IWF.
Each manager is single-strategy, so its 13F book should track 1 mutual fund; that is tested, not assumed.
A fund passes if its quarterly book return correlates with its NAV return at 0.90 or more over the window.
A failed fund keeps its Table 4 row and is left out of Tables 1 to 3, Charts 1 to 3 and the answers.
